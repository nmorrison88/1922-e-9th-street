History & Location

A Timeless
Tucson Address

Built in 1910, this remarkable residence is part of Tucson’s historic Iron Horse District, an enclave established for some of the city’s earliest railroaders.

Originally built for Peter and Mary Ziegler, with Peter Ziegler recognized as Tucson’s first railroad engineer, the home carries a rich legacy of the city’s early growth, architecture, and community. Today, it offers a rare opportunity to own a piece of Tucson history, thoughtfully updated for modern living.
